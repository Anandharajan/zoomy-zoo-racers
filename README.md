# 🏁 Zoomy Zoo Racers

A kid-safe arcade racing game in **one HTML file**. Pick an animal driver, collect stars, fill the Turbo Meter and zoom past friendly rivals through three bright worlds. Bumps go *boing* — nothing ever crashes.

## ▶️ Play now

**https://anandharajan.github.io/zoomy-zoo-racers/**

Works on desktop, phones and tablets. On a phone, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" to launch it like an app.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Touch | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Steer | ← → / A D | ◀ ▶ buttons | Left stick / D-pad |
| Accelerate | ↑ / W | automatic | A / RT |
| Brake | ↓ / S | 🛑 | B / LT |
| Drift | Shift | 🌀 | X |
| Turbo | Space | 🚀 | Y |
| Pause | P / Esc | ⏸ | Start |
| Mute | M | 🔊 | — |

## 👨‍👩‍👧 For parents

- **No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no chat, no network requests.** The whole game is one file that runs in the browser.
- **No violence.** Bumping into things makes a funny *boing* and a brief slowdown — no crashes, damage or scary content.
- **🐢 Calm mode** (always visible) lowers the speed and removes screen shake and flashing for younger or motion-sensitive players.
- The only thing saved is the best lap times, on your own device.

## 🤝 Contribute

Fork it, tweak the numbers in the `CONFIG` object at the top of `index.html`, and open a PR. Everything — cars, worlds, sound — is drawn and synthesised in code; there are no asset files.
